# Just These Things — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: April 29, 2026*

## Summary

Just These Things does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on third-party servers. The few things you write live in your own iCloud, on your own devices.

## What the app accesses

**iCloud (CloudKit)** — Used to sync the contents of your slots between your iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch. Sync uses your private CloudKit database; the data is never visible to the developer or to other users. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can disable sync for this app at any time in iOS Settings → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud.

**Siri & Shortcuts (App Intents)** — Used to set, read, and clear your slots by voice or via the Shortcuts app. Siri requests are processed under [Apple's Siri privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/siri/). The app itself sees only the resolved text you spoke; it does not transmit anything to a server.

**Apple Watch (Watch Connectivity)** — Used to mirror the current slot contents to the watch app and complications. The watch projection contains only the same slot text that's already in your iCloud database. No additional data is collected.

## What the app does not do

- No user accounts or sign-in
- No analytics or crash reporting
- No third-party SDKs that collect data
- No advertising
- No location, health, calendar, or contacts access
- No data shared with the developer or any third party

## Data stored on your device

Just These Things stores your slot contents and slot configuration in a local SwiftData database, and (if iCloud sync is enabled) in your private CloudKit database. The data is not accessible to other apps and is removed from your device when you delete the app. iCloud copies are removed according to Apple's standard iCloud retention policy.
